About Us

Crafting Spaces That Inspire

A leading provider of architectural and interior design solutions

Crekaar has established itself as a leading provider of architectural and interior design solutions. We created the company out of a passion for design and a desire to create spaces that inspire and enrich our clients' lives. Our journey began with a vision to redefine the way people experience their living environments.

We bring together expertise from various disciplines, including architecture, interior design, custom furniture, project management, and construction. Our collaborative approach ensures that every project is executed with precision and attention to detail, resulting in exceptional design solutions that exceed our client's expectations.

We are committed to innovation, creativity, and excellence in everything we do. Whether it's designing a modern residence, renovating a space, or creating a stylish commercial space, we approach each project with enthusiasm and dedication.

Our Process

From Vision to Reality

A meticulously structured design and construction process ensuring exceptional outcomes

01

Initial Consultation

We meet with you to discuss your vision, goals, and budget, gaining a thorough understanding of your needs and establishing clear objectives.

02

Concept Development

Our designers develop conceptual designs that capture the essence of your vision, presenting initial concepts for your feedback and refinement.

03

Design Development

We refine the details, create detailed drawings, select materials and finishes, and coordinate with engineers and consultants.

04

Documentation

Comprehensive construction documents are prepared outlining technical requirements and specifications as a blueprint for construction.

05

Construction

Our team oversees implementation, coordinating with contractors and suppliers to ensure the highest standards of quality and craftsmanship.

06

Completion & Handover

A final inspection ensures every detail meets our exacting standards before handing over the completed project, ready for you to enjoy.
